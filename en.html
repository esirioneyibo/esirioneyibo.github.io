About

I am a multidisciplinary software developer and Automation Engineering graduate with a passion for technology, entrepreneurship and practical problem-solving.

After graduating in Electrical and Automation Engineering in 2013, I began learning software development independently. My journey started with HTML, CSS and web design and expanded into Python, Ruby on Rails, Flutter, React Native, Next.js, database systems and cloud deployment.

I have worked on logistics platforms, business applications, algorithmic trading systems, mobile applications, productivity software and automation tools. I founded Nadaruns to connect available transport capacity with businesses requiring delivery services.

My broader background includes industrial work, healthcare-related studies and experience, youth services and early childhood education. These experiences influence how I design technology around real human and business needs. My goal is to continue developing useful, reliable and accessible technology products.

Languages: English (native); Finnish: good spoken, satisfactory written. Outside technology: volunteering, outdoor activities and hiking with family.
